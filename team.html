Core team

Meet the team building ZIMA.

Subvision brings together mechanical design, electronics, software, and venture development around one job: building a practical system for prevention-first hull maintenance.

Talk to the team See partner momentum

Subvision started with a group of us at Simon Fraser University who wanted to build underwater robots. We created a marine engineering club to make it happen. Building a robot together took us to the MATE ROV World Championship in Tennessee, but it also made us want to do something beyond competitions. We wanted to take what we’d learned and put it toward a problem that mattered to the ocean and the people who work on it.

Today, we’re a Vancouver-based team building ZIMA from the ground up. We bring different strengths in mechanical design, electronics, power systems, and robotics software, but the work is rarely neatly divided. We help each other troubleshoot, challenge each other’s ideas, and learn together when a design doesn’t behave the way we expected. From the custom underwater drive system to the controls that guide ZIMA across a ship’s hull, this is something we’re making together. We’re proud of how far we’ve come, and we’re building toward a goal we all care about: helping ships stay efficient while protecting the waters they travel through.
